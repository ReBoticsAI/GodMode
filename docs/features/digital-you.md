# Digital You

![digital-you in GodMode](/features/digital-you.png)


**Digital You** is your digital twin, created on first login. It is a **private thinking partner** and a **stand-in** for your perspective when you are unavailable. It is distinct from Intelligence and from department subagents.

Stand-in quality **compounds with use**: Reflection, profile, and saved memories build a model of how you think and decide. It is usable immediately as a sounding board; it is not a finished auto-coverage product on day one. Deeper stand-in automation is tracked under roadmap #79.

The product name is **Digital You** (not “Digital user”).

## Where to open

Sidebar → **Digital** (your display name)

## Agent notes

- Do not conflate Digital You with Intelligence.
- Route personal judgment, voice, and stand-in work here.
- Route platform setup, structure mutation, and plugin build to Intelligence ([[intelligence]]).
- Treat replies as the user’s perspective draft, not irreversible authority without human/Authority bounds.
