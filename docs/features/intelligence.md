# Intelligence

![intelligence in GodMode](/features/intelligence.png)


**Intelligence** is the built-in **platform agent**. It is the nervous system: talk to it to grow GodMode and its anatomy (structure, agents, wiki, plugins and cross-cutting ops) from chat.

## Where to open

Chat panel → agent picker → **Intelligence**

## What it is not

Intelligence is not the product name and not the model (the brain). **GodMode** is the body. Intelligence is the agent you talk to in order to grow it.

## Agent notes

- Prefer Intelligence for platform setup, structure, coding/plugins and cross-cutting ops.
- Use Digital You for your twin: voice, conventions, guidance and stand-in ([[digital-you]]).
- Specialized agents own a job, attach to a region and execute ([[agents]]).
- Self-expansion loop: workspace tools → `scaffold_plugin` → build/install → Marketplace when sharing packs ([[plugin-pipeline]], [[marketplace]]).
